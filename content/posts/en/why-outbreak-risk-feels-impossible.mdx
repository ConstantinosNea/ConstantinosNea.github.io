People don’t struggle with risk because they are irrational. They struggle because the information environment is incomplete, delayed, and emotionally charged.

## Risk is a range, not a headline

A single case count without context is not a decision tool. Ask:

- How complete is testing and reporting?
- Who is most exposed right now?
- What actions change outcomes this week?

## Communication that helps

Clear public health writing names uncertainty, separates individual and population risk, and ends with one actionable next step.

When risk feels impossible to gauge, demand better questions — not louder certainty.
