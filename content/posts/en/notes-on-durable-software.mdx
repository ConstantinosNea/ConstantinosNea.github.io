Frameworks age. Constraints remain. The work that ages well is usually the work that made fewer promises and kept more of them.

## Prefer boring boundaries

A durable system does not need novelty in every layer. Put creativity where users feel it. Keep the seams predictable so people can rotate onto the team without folklore.

## Explainability is a feature

If you cannot sketch the architecture on a whiteboard in five minutes, you do not have an architecture — you have an accumulation.

Ship the smallest complete change that leaves the codebase easier to narrate than before.
