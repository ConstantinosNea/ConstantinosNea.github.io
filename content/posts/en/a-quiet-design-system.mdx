Most interfaces shout. A quiet design system whispers—and people lean in.

## Hierarchy before decoration

Typography does the heavy lifting. Establish three sizes, two weights, and a single serif for emphasis. Color is optional; structure is not.

Whitespace is not empty. It is pacing. It tells the reader where to pause and what matters next.

## Monochrome as a feature

A grayscale palette reduces decision fatigue for both designer and reader. Contrast becomes intentional:

1. Near-black for primary text
2. Mid-gray for metadata
3. Soft borders only where separation is required

When everything competes for attention, nothing wins. When almost nothing competes, the words do.
