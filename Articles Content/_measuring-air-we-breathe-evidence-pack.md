## Do not claim
- That fixed monitors systematically underestimate personal exposure everywhere
- That FAIRMODE guidance alone changes population health outcomes
- Invented hotspot magnitudes
- EU = WHO European Region

## Charts
- Callout: ≤20% stations above current EU standards (Q1)
- Columns: PM2.5 annual WHO 5 / EU 2030 10 / EU current 25 (Q3) — standards comparison, not measured exposures
- Conceptual package (labelled conceptual, not empirical): ambient concentration → spatial representativeness → population/personal exposure
