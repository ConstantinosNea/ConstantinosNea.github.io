## Do not claim / easy overreach
- Do not treat e-cigarettes, pouches, HTPs and cigarettes as equivalent risk.
- Do not present global unsafe-abortion-style mistakes: keep ESPAD (15–16) distinct from WHO 13–15 estimates.
- Do not claim long-term chronic disease risk of vaping is fully known.
- Do not claim all youth vapers would have become smokers (gateway is debated; frame as population risk of nicotine-naïve initiation).
- Do not cite NASEM 2018 cessation conclusions as current over Cochrane living review.
- Do not use US NYTS youth % as European figures.

## Conflicts assessed
- WHO 13–15 e-cig 14.3% vs ESPAD 15–16 current 22%: different age, instruments, countries — use both with labels, never merge.
- Cochrane high-certainty cessation benefit vs CDC/USPSTF caution and WHO youth-protection framing: complementary, not contradictory — different populations/questions.
