# Evidence review: nicotine-beyond-smoking

## Verdict
Pass

## Summary
Major quantitative claims were re-checked against WHO/Europe WNTD 2026, ESPAD/EUDA 2024 key findings, Cochrane living review (searches to 1 Jan 2026), CDC e-cigarette health-effects page (Jan 2025), and the European Commission tobacco-control evaluation executive summary (SWD(2026) 112, April 2026). Geography and age bands are kept distinct (WHO 13–15 vs ESPAD 15–16). Charts plot only verified register figures. Harm-reduction and youth-initiation framings are attributed separately; long-term uncertainty is stated. Final revision adds Begh et al. 2025 (*Addiction*) for the youth vaping–later smoking association with explicit uncertainty, and Cochrane CD016220 for limited/uncertain nicotine-pouch cessation and long-term evidence — both cited in References and attached in prose.
