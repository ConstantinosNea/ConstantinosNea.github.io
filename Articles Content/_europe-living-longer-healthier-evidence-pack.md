## Do not claim
- Do not subtract HLY from LE and label remainder “years in poor health” without method qualification.
- Do not equate HALE (2019 Cyprus) with Eurostat HLY (2024 EU) in one ranking.
- Do not treat Cyprus high home-care coverage as proof of affordable integrated LTC.
