## Frame
- Outcome: gap between legal entitlement and practical access to safe, timely, affordable abortion care
- Population: people seeking abortion care (primarily women/girls; WHO inclusive language)
- Place: Europe / EU Member States + selected national examples
- Time: WHO guideline 2nd ed Aug 2025; WHO fact sheet Dec 2025; EPRS Sep 2025; Italy MoH/ISS data year 2023; CoE Res 1607 (2008, still cited); Bearak 2015–19 estimates

**Epi essential** for global unsafe-abortion burden and Europe/Northern America abortion incidence context. Access barriers and legal mapping are major quantitative/policy claims needing register rows.

## Conflicts / caveats
- Global unsafe-abortion share (45%) is not a Europe-specific rate; Europe generally has high procedural safety where services are available — argue association carefully.
- Abortion rates do not equal “need” or “access quality”; laws do not map linearly onto rates (European Journal of Contraception 2022).
- CRR/EPF are advocacy-linked comparative tools — prefer WHO/EPRS/national stats for majors; CRR only for transparent legal-count illustration.
- Italy objector % ≠ share of active providers (methodological caveat in literature).
