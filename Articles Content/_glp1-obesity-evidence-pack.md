# Article evidence pack — glp1-obesity-who-pays

Updated: 2026-10-12 (revision refresh)  
Mode: Article support (epidemiology-research + trial/policy verification)

## Frame
- Outcome: obesity treatment access, efficacy, CV risk reduction, prioritisation
- Population: adults with obesity (WHO BMI ≥30 for guideline; trials also BMI ≥27 + comorbidity)
- Place: global WHO + European EMA/HTA context
- Time: trials 2021–2026; WHO guideline Dec 2025; WHO allocation Expert Group 2026→guidance early 2027

## Necessity
**Epi essential** for prevalence/burden; major trial outcomes and WHO access projection are claim-register majors.

## Conflicts / caveats
- EMA MA broader (BMI≥27+comorbidity) than WHO obesity recommendation (BMI≥30).
- SELECT is high-CV-risk secondary prevention, not all people with obesity.
- Tirzepatide MACE outcomes still emerging (SURMOUNT-MMO pending ~2027 per HAS).
- Industry funding of STEP/SELECT/SURMOUNT: disclose; judge on design/methods.
